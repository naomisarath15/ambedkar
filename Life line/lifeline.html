About Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

Dr. Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar (14 April 1891 – 6 December 1956) was an Indian jurist, economist, social reformer, and politician who served as the Chairman of the Constitution Drafting Committee and free India's first Minister of Law and Justice.

Having earned doctorates in economics from both Columbia University and the London School of Economics, and trained as a barrister at Gray's Inn, he spearheaded the struggle for social emancipation and human rights for India's oppressed classes.

In 1990, Dr. Ambedkar was posthumously awarded the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honor.
