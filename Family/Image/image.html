FIRST WIFE & PILLAR OF SACRIFICE

Matoshree Ramabai Ambedkar

1898 — 1935
"Ramabai endured extreme penury, personal loss, and prolonged separation in silence, enabling Babasaheb to complete his higher studies abroad."

Affectionately called 'Ramai', Babasaheb dedicated his monumental book Thoughts on Pakistan to her, revering her goodness of heart, nobility of mind, and silent suffering during his early struggles.
